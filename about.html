About

Built by a road tripper, for road trippers.

Kibi started on I-65, somewhere between Atlanta and Chicago, with a full bladder, an empty stomach, and the stubborn belief that there had to be a better way.

Brett Moran

Founder, Kibi · Jacksonville, FL

By day, Brett is a Senior Associate at Alvarez & Marsal in their Disputes & Investigations / Forensic practice, where he digs through data for Fortune 500 clients. By night, he's the founder and sole builder behind Kibi.

The idea for Kibi started on I-65, somewhere between Atlanta and Chicago — a full bladder, an empty stomach, and the stubborn belief that current navigation apps don't actually help you pick where to stop. Two years of planning and a few months of nonstop building later, Kibi scores 79,000+ U.S. highway exits to find the single best pitstop on any route.

Brett holds a BBA in Management Information Systems from the University of Georgia (2019). He's based in Jacksonville, Florida, where he road tests Kibi against real highway corridors most weekends.
